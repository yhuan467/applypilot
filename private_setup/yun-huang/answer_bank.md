# Yun Huang Answer Bank

Candidate Profile stores facts. This file stores reusable wording.

## Work Authorization

Default answer:

I am legally authorized to work in the United States.

When to ask:

- The form wording is unusual or more specific than this sentence.
- The form asks about document type, expiration, or a future timeline not covered by the stored profile.

## Sponsorship

Default answer:

I do not require sponsorship now or in the future.

When to ask:

- The wording differs from the stored profile.
- The form combines work authorization, sponsorship, and immigration timing into one question.

## Location

I am currently based in Tempe, Arizona, and I am open to roles across the United States.

Relocation answer:

I am open to relocation based on the role and team.

Remote/hybrid/onsite answer:

I am open to remote, hybrid, and onsite opportunities.

## Compensation

Default range:

My target base salary range is $70,000-$125,000, depending on role scope and total package.

Deferral wording:

- The form requires a number outside the stored range.

Strategy:
