# Yun Huang Application Rules

## Mode

Selected mode: Volume

Use stable resume variants for most roles. Promote a specific high-fit role to Precision if the role is unusually strong and worth custom tailoring.

## First Trial Boundary

Selected first trial boundary: Lead finding only

For the first trial:

- Find 3-5 jobs.
- Screen and classify them.
- Update the dashboard.
- Do not open application flows.
- Do not click Apply.
- Do not submit applications.

## Prioritize

Apply quickly when a job matches:

- Role families: Analyst, Software Engineer, ML Engineer, AI Infrastructure, Full Stack Engineer.
- Titles: entry-level analyst, data analyst, business analyst, quantitative analyst, software engineer, new grad software engineer, junior software engineer, ML engineer, machine learning engineer, AI infrastructure engineer, full stack engineer.
- Level: entry-level, new grad, associate, junior, university graduate, early career.
- Freshness: posted in the last 24 hours first, then 48 hours.
- Locations: Tempe first when available, then Phoenix Metro, Remote US, and relocation-friendly US roles.
- Form length: short ATS flows, LinkedIn Easy Apply, and low-friction company sites first.

## Consider

Review before applying when:

- The role asks for 1-3 years but the work still looks realistic for a strong new grad.
- The title is adjacent, such as data engineer, solutions engineer, research engineer, backend engineer, infrastructure engineer, or systems-oriented software engineer, and the content still fits the resume.
- The role is outside Arizona and not clearly remote, but relocation is possible.
- Compensation is not listed.
- The role is high-fit but long-form.
- The role is a slight stretch, but the user explicitly wants realistic high-upside roles included when they still look attainable for a strong new graduate.

## Skip

Default skip when:

- Title includes Senior, Staff, Principal, Lead, Manager, Director, or above.
- Required experience is clearly 4+ years and not written as flexible.
- The role is internship-only, contract-only, commission-only, or unrelated to the target role families.
- The role requires a long writing sample, video submission, or portfolio artifact before interview.
- The posting is closed, duplicate, or already applied.
- The role is not US-based and does not fit the stored work authorization context.

## Hand Off to User

Stop and ask the user when:

- Work authorization wording differs from the stored profile.
- Compensation requested is outside $70,000-$125,000.
- CAPTCHA, Cloudflare, login, or 2FA appears.
- Resume upload cannot be verified.
- A form requires a portfolio, custom writing sample, or other missing material.
- The final submission is ready for review.

## Default Form Behavior

- Fill clear basic fields automatically from Candidate Profile.
- Use stored work authorization wording only when the form meaning clearly matches it.
- Leave voluntary self-ID blank or choose "Prefer not to say" when the form allows it.
- Draft custom answers from Answer Bank patterns, then ask the user to confirm if a pattern does not already exist.
- Always stop before final submit with a short summary.

## Low-Friction Priority

Favor these first in volume mode:

- LinkedIn
- Simplify
- Company sites with short forms
- Handshake

Deprioritize these unless the fit is unusually strong:

- Workday
- Oracle
- Account-heavy enterprise ATS flows
- Long custom applications

## Status Classification

- `Pending`: worth later review or application, with no known high-impact blocker.
- `Needs user`: missing user-owned fact or action blocks the decision.
- `Skipped`: does not match the rules or is not worth applying.
- `Blocked`: attempted workflow could not safely proceed.
- `Submitted`: explicit confirmation evidence was observed.

## Account Policy

Preferred sources:

- LinkedIn
- Company sites
- Simplify
- Handshake

If a different account or identity appears, stop and ask.
