## Fit Signals

If no external score exists, use qualitative fit.

| Signal | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Role/title match | Strongest for analyst, software engineer, ML engineer, AI infrastructure, and full stack roles. |
| Level and years match | Prioritize entry-level, new grad, associate, or junior roles. |
| Skill/keyword match | Python, SQL, JavaScript, data pipelines, dashboards, RL/ML, APIs, full-stack delivery. |
| Domain/industry match | Flexible; do not over-filter by industry until more results are observed. |
| Location/work authorization feasibility | Tempe preferred, but remote and relocation-friendly US roles are valid. |
| Candidate story coherence | CS new grad with both analyst and technical projects; choose the resume that makes the story easiest to understand. |
| Form complexity vs value | Long forms only when the role is clearly strong-fit. |

Fit labels:

- High: strong role match, entry-level, realistic skill match, and reasonable form complexity.
- Medium: mostly relevant but slightly stretchy on title, location, or years.
- Low: weak alignment or noisy job description.
- Stretch: visibly above level or skill profile.

## Tailoring Rules

Tailor only when:

- Fit is High.
- The company or role is especially valuable.
- The role is worth slowing down for.
- An editable resume source exists or the user approves creating one.

Do not tailor when:

- A stable resume variant is already a strong fit.
- The role is only Medium or Stretch.
- The form is long and the conversion probability is low.
